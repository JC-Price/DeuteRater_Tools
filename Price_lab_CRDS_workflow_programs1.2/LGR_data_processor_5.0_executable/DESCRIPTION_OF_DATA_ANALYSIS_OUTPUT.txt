Asymptote calculation graphs
- These graphs show the exponential approach of each injection-measurement cycle (8 per sample) towards the true 
deuterium enrichment. The "true enrichment" is calculated by matching the values to an exponential fit with an
asymptote. The asymptote is the "true enrichment". When the injection-measurement cycle curve fails to fit an 
exponential fit (usually due to there being a very small measurement range), the "true enrichment" is given by 
the average of the final three measurements.

-This plot shows the deuterium enrichment for each time point.
